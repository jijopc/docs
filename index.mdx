## How to Use This Guide

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Safety Guidelines First">
    Before performing any maintenance, read the [Safety Guidelines](/safety-guidelines) to understand lockout/tagout procedures, PPE requirements, and refrigerant handling protocols.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Maintenance Schedule">
    Use the [Maintenance Schedules](/maintenance-schedules) page to identify which tasks are due based on the service interval (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow Equipment-Specific Procedures">
    Navigate to the relevant equipment section — Cooling Systems or Plant & Equipment — and follow the step-by-step maintenance procedures for your unit type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete the Checklist">
    Use the printable [Daily](/checklists/daily-checklist), [Monthly](/checklists/monthly-checklist), or [Annual](/checklists/annual-checklist) checklists to document completed tasks and sign off on service records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Always consult the original equipment manufacturer (OEM) manual for model-specific torque values, refrigerant charge specifications, and warranty requirements. This guide provides general industry-standard procedures.
</Note>

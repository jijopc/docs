---

Monthly preventive maintenance goes beyond the quick instrument rounds of your daily checks. These visits involve hands-on cleaning, mechanical inspection, and performance verification tasks that typically require 2 to 4 hours per equipment type depending on plant complexity. Completing monthly PM consistently extends equipment life, maintains efficiency, and reduces the likelihood of unplanned breakdowns between quarterly or annual service visits.

<Warning>
  Before starting any monthly maintenance task, ensure all relevant lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures are followed, appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) is worn, and permits to work are issued where required. Never work on energised equipment or live refrigerant circuits without authorisation and appropriate training.
</Warning>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Chiller System">
    **Estimated visit time: 2–3 hours**

    - [ ] Inspect and clean chiller evaporator and condenser barrel exteriors; check for water staining or corrosion
    - [ ] Check all chilled water and condenser water pipe joints, flanges, and valve glands for leaks; tighten packing as needed
    - [ ] Perform refrigerant leak check on full chiller circuit using calibrated electronic leak detector; log result
    - [ ] Record and compare chiller efficiency (kW/TR or COP) against baseline commissioning data
    - [ ] Inspect and test all safety cut-outs: high-pressure switch, low-pressure switch, oil pressure differential switch, and motor overloads
    - [ ] Clean condenser water strainer basket and check strainer housing O-ring condition
    - [ ] Inspect chilled water expansion vessel pre-charge pressure (compare to system static head; recharge if < 0.5 bar of design)
    - [ ] Check anti-vibration mounts and spring isolators on chiller and pumps for deterioration or compression set
    - [ ] Inspect all control wiring in the chiller panel; check for chafe marks, loose terminals, or signs of overheating
    - [ ] Review BMS trend data for the past month; identify any exceedances of temperature, pressure, or amp draw limits
    - [ ] Lubricate chilled water pump bearing housings per manufacturer schedule (grease nipple, correct grease grade)
    - [ ] Test chiller auto-changeover and duty/standby sequencing function
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="AHU">
    **Estimated visit time: 1.5–2.5 hours per unit**

    - [ ] Replace or clean AHU panel filters (G3/G4 pre-filters); record filter differential pressure before and after
    - [ ] Inspect bag or cassette secondary filters (F7/F9); replace if ΔP exceeds manufacturer's change-out limit
    - [ ] Clean cooling and heating coil fins with low-pressure compressed air or approved fin comb; inspect for damage
    - [ ] Inspect and clean condensate drain pan — remove biofilm, check for standing water, flush drain with diluted biocide
    - [ ] Lubricate supply and return fan shaft bearings per manufacturer schedule
    - [ ] Check fan belt tension and condition: deflection should not exceed 10–15 mm per 300 mm span; inspect for glazing or cracking
    - [ ] Check and tighten all fan and motor mounting bolts; verify drive alignment is within tolerance
    - [ ] Inspect motorised dampers (fresh air, return air, mixing) — operate through full stroke and verify blade sealing
    - [ ] Check and calibrate room or duct-mounted temperature and humidity sensors against calibrated reference instrument
    - [ ] Inspect AHU drain valve and humidifier (where fitted) for scale build-up; descale per manufacturer guidance
    - [ ] Review AHU fault log on BMS; clear historical alarms and document recurring faults for investigation
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="DX Split AC">
    **Estimated visit time: 45–90 minutes per system**

    - [ ] Clean indoor unit washable air filters — wash with warm water, allow to dry fully before reinstalling
    - [ ] Inspect indoor unit evaporator coil: check for ice formation, dirt fouling, or corrosion; clean with approved coil cleaner if needed
    - [ ] Flush condensate drain tray and pipe with diluted biocide solution; confirm free drainage
    - [ ] Check indoor unit fan blower wheel for dust accumulation; clean with brush and vacuum
    - [ ] Inspect interconnecting refrigerant pipe insulation for damage, UV degradation, or moisture ingress
    - [ ] Check outdoor unit condenser coil: remove debris, straighten bent fins with fin comb, verify airflow is unobstructed
    - [ ] Inspect outdoor unit fan blade for cracks, balance, and blade pitch; check motor mounts
    - [ ] Check outdoor unit electrical connections at contactor and terminal block; tighten to manufacturer torque spec
    - [ ] Measure and record system operating pressures (suction and discharge) against expected values for refrigerant type
    - [ ] Verify system achieves set point within 15 minutes of start-up from ambient temperature
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Package AC (Water/Air-Cooled)">
    **Estimated visit time: 2–3 hours per unit**

    - [ ] Inspect and clean supply air filters; record differential pressure before and after filter service
    - [ ] Clean evaporator coil with low-pressure water spray or approved no-rinse coil cleaner; inspect for corrosion
    - [ ] Inspect and clean condensate drain pan; flush drain pipework with biocide; confirm drainage rate
    - [ ] Check and record refrigerant operating pressures on all circuits; compare to expected values for refrigerant type and load
    - [ ] For water-cooled units: inspect condenser water connections and record entering/leaving temperatures; clean strainer basket
    - [ ] For air-cooled units: inspect condenser coil face, clean fins, and verify condenser fan operation
    - [ ] Check compressor oil level (sight glass) and oil condition (colour, clarity); record findings
    - [ ] Inspect all electrical connections in control panel; check contactor contacts for pitting or burning
    - [ ] Test capacity control stages (hot-gas bypass, unloader, VFD) through BMS demand signals
    - [ ] Check and test all safety devices: high-pressure cut-out, low-pressure cut-out, and phase-failure relay
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Refrigeration Plant">
    **Estimated visit time: 2–4 hours depending on system complexity**

    - [ ] Perform full refrigerant leak check on all circuits using electronic leak detector; record results in F-gas log
    - [ ] Record and calculate superheat and sub-cooling on each circuit; compare to design values
    - [ ] Check and top up compressor oil if below minimum sight glass level; use correct grade per manufacturer specification
    - [ ] Inspect oil separator (where fitted) for oil carry-over; drain and inspect at sight glass
    - [ ] Clean condenser coil(s) and check condenser fan operation; measure air temperature rise across coil
    - [ ] Inspect and clean evaporator — check for ice build-up, frost patterns, or evidence of liquid flooding
    - [ ] Initiate and observe manual defrost cycle; confirm defrost termination and drain operation
    - [ ] Test all safety cut-outs: high-pressure, low-pressure, oil pressure differential, and over-temperature
    - [ ] Check expansion device operation: inspect TXV / EEV bulb securing clamps and insulation; verify superheat control
    - [ ] Inspect all refrigerant pipework supports and hangers; check for vibration damage at pipe clamps
    - [ ] Review controller fault log; document any high-pressure trips, low-pressure events, or sensor alarms
    - [ ] Check door seals and gaskets on refrigerated enclosures for air leaks and damage
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Condenser & Compressor">
    **Estimated visit time: 1.5–2.5 hours**

    **Condenser Tasks**

    - [ ] Clean air-cooled condenser coil — blow out fins with compressed air (always blow in opposite direction to airflow); straighten damaged fins
    - [ ] Inspect condenser fan motors: check for moisture ingress, measure motor winding resistance (should be > 1 MΩ to earth)
    - [ ] Check condenser fan blade tip clearance and confirm blades are secure on motor shaft
    - [ ] For evaporative/water-cooled condensers: test water treatment dosing levels (biocide, corrosion inhibitor, scale inhibitor); record results
    - [ ] Clean cooling tower or evaporative condenser basin, sump, and spray nozzles

    **Compressor Tasks**

    - [ ] Check compressor crankcase oil level and colour — oil should be clear to light amber; dark or foamy oil indicates contamination
    - [ ] Measure and record compressor suction and discharge temperatures as well as pressures on all cylinders/circuits
    - [ ] Check crankcase heater function: heater should be warm to touch during standby; confirm amps with clamp meter
    - [ ] Inspect compressor discharge and suction service valves — confirm backseat position and check valve cap O-rings
    - [ ] Check all compressor mounting bolts and anti-vibration pads; tighten to specification and replace deteriorated pads
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
